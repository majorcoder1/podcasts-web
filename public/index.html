Everything the old one did

Built to replace Google Podcasts after it shut down in 2024 — the same three-tab layout and the same habits, running on plain RSS.

Any feed, no gatekeeper

Search the directory, or paste an RSS URL directly. If a show publishes a feed, it plays here.

Never lose your place

Pause on the phone at the bus stop, pick it up in the browser at the same second.

A real queue

Add to the end, or jump something to the front with Play next. It plays straight through.

Listen offline

Download episodes on Wi-Fi and play them with no signal. They clear themselves once you're done.

Speed and sleep timer

Half speed to triple. Sleep timer with the usual presets, or stop at the end of the episode.

Bring your list with you

Import an OPML export from any other app — including your old Google Podcasts export — in one go.

Podcasts for Android

Requires Android 7.0 or newer. Plays in the background with lock-screen controls.

Download APK
  1. Tap Download APK. Your browser will warn you that this kind of file can harm your device — that warning appears for every APK, including this one.
  2. Open the downloaded file. Android will ask whether to allow installs from your browser. Turn it on for that app only.
  3. Install, then sign in with the same account you use here, and your subscriptions arrive on their own.

One account, both devices

Your account exists so your listening can follow you. Subscriptions, queue and playback position sync between the phone app and this site.

Accounts are on this server only. Nothing is shared with anyone, there is no advertising ID, and no third party is told what you listen to.
